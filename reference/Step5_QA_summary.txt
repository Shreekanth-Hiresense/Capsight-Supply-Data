--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   master hold 231,157 current profiles (5.2% of the per-company current sum) and count toward NO
   L1/L2. Their top names are squarely in the short sectors (Maersk/DHL -> Logistics; EXL/
   Teleperformance/Virtusa/CGI/Randstad -> Consulting; AGS Health -> Healthcare; Target -> Retail).
   => Classifying these would lift exactly the short industries. They are FLAGGED, not guessed
   (Companies_Needing_Industry_Review.csv, with LinkedIn URLs, in Downloads).
2. PART-2 11-FUNCTION RESTRICTION (deliberate, per instruction): Part-2 companies' CURRENT is
   limited to the 371,165 in-11-function uids. Part-2 skews to Consulting/Healthcare/Industrial,
   so this intentionally depresses those industry totals vs an unrestricted benchmark. Not an error.
3. CONNECTION FILTER conn>=20: uniform suppression across all buckets; minor, applied for quality.
   After 1-3, Chennai/Tamil Nadu (54-57%) remains the clearest SOURCING-side gap -> likely under-
   coverage of Chennai-centred GCCs (auto OEMs, manufacturing, healthcare). Worth a targeted add.

E. ASSUMPTIONS REQUIRING VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Comparability: "ours" = distinct people CURRENTLY at a target company whose OWN profile location
   is city X. The GCC-master target is headcount at the company's GCC CENTRE in city X. These are
   close but not identical (a person may live in the city yet not be posted to that company's centre,
   or vice-versa). Treat ratios as directional, not exact.
2. Delhi-NCR grouping: Gurugram+Noida+Delhi folded into one city line and one state line, per the
   standing rule. Lucknow is kept separate under Uttar Pradesh (UP target reduced to its non-Noida
   remainder, 470). Haryana and NCT-of-Delhi state rows are folded into "Delhi-NCR (grouped)".
3. Targets are MINIMUM benchmarks: buckets at >=100% (BFSI 123%, Software 106%, Telecom 142%, many
   small cities) are reported OK with no action; being over target is expected and not inflated -
   counts are distinct-uid, so there is no double-counting producing the overage.
4. Part-2 current = 11 functions only (standing instruction); overall/ever counts are NOT function-
   filtered. Changing either choice would move the industry and city numbers.
5. Industry for the 195 unclassified companies is BLANK by design (flagged), so current industry
   totals are a floor; they will rise once those are classified.
